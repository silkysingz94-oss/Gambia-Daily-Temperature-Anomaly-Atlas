METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Anomaly = daily ERA5-Land maximum temperature minus the 1991–2020 mean for the same calendar day. Values are extracted at 30 fixed sample coordinates and do not represent a continuous high-resolution surface. This is a model-based screening product, not an official GMet bulletin.
